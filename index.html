Our commitment

Kalupation ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application and related services. Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using Kalupation, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal information that is necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account data: when you create an account, we may collect your email address and a unique identifier.
  • Usage data: anonymized interaction statistics (features used, clicks, session duration) to enhance user experience.
  • Device data: basic technical information such as operating system version, device type, and approximate location (only if you grant permission).
  • Support communications: if you contact us via email, we retain the content of your messages to resolve issues.

We do not collect sensitive categories of personal data (health, race, religion, etc.).

2. How we use your information
  • To provide, operate, and maintain Kalupation features.
  • To improve personalization and interface performance.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraud.
  • To send essential service notifications (not marketing) if applicable.

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing & disclosure

We only share your data in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist in hosting, analytics, or crash reporting (bound by confidentiality).
  • If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition — with notice to you.

We may share aggregated, de-identified data for research or product development.

4. Data security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. While no method is 100% secure, we strive to protect your information with appropriate technical and organizational measures.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your rights & choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Kalupation is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in certain regions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. For significant changes, we will provide a prominent notice within the app or via email.
